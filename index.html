Questions

Solar, without the jargon.

How much roof area do I need?

It depends on panel technology, layout, setbacks and shading. A rough planning figure is useful, but a site assessment is needed before final sizing.

Will solar completely eliminate my bill?

Not necessarily. Your result depends on system size, consumption, generation, export/import rules, fixed charges and other DISCOM factors.

Can businesses use solar?

Yes, commercial and industrial solar is possible, but economics, metering, financing and support can differ from residential systems.

What happens after I submit the form?

Your details can be routed to Narla Solar Panel Cost Analyzer through the included Google Apps Script setup. Once connected, the enquiry can be stored in Google Sheets and emailed to the business owner. Automatic messaging alerts require a separately configured messaging service.

How long does a rooftop solar installation usually take?

The timeline depends on site assessment, system design, equipment availability, approvals and installation. We will give you an expected timeline after reviewing your requirement.

Does my roof need to face a particular direction?

Roof orientation, tilt and shading affect solar generation. A site assessment helps determine the practical panel layout for your property.

What happens if my roof has shade?

Shade from trees, nearby buildings or structures can reduce generation. A proper site assessment should identify shaded areas before final system sizing.

Can I install solar if I live in an apartment?

It depends on roof access, ownership or association permissions, metering arrangements and the building's electrical setup. We can discuss your situation before recommending a system.

What maintenance does a solar system need?

Solar systems generally require periodic cleaning and basic inspection. Monitoring generation and keeping panels reasonably clean can help maintain performance.

Can solar power be used for an EV charger?

Yes. Solar can be planned alongside future EV charging, although the required system size depends on your household consumption and charging pattern.

Can I expand my solar system later?

Expansion depends on your inverter, roof space, electrical infrastructure and applicable rules. It is useful to discuss future electricity needs before the first installation.

Do you provide a quotation before installation?

Yes. After understanding your electricity usage, property and roof conditions, a quotation can be prepared based on the proposed system and installation requirements.

What information should I keep ready for a solar consultation?

Your recent electricity bill, approximate roof information, property type and location are useful starting points. Additional documents may be requested later if required for the installation process.
